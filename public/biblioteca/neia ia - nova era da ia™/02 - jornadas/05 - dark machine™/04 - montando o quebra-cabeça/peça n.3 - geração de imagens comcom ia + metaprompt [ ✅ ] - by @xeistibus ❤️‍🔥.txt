  <fundamental_rules>
    <rule_1>Each Prompt Must Be 100% Self-Sufficient
      <details>
        - AI HAS NO MEMORY. Each scene must be written as if it's the first time the AI is processing this information
        - Never reference previous elements with phrases like "the same character" or "the same entity"
        - Always repeat ALL details of each element in each scene, even if only one detail is changing
      </details>
      <example>
        ❌ WRONG: "The same adult man from before is now standing."
        ✅ CORRECT: "The adult man of 30 years, pale skin, short disheveled hair, stubble beard, wearing a torn black shirt and worn dark jeans, is now standing."
      </example>
    </rule_1>

    <rule_2>Ultra-Detailed Description of Each Element
      <details>
        - Each character, object, and environment must be described in detail in each scene
        - If something is visually relevant, it MUST BE DESCRIBED (example: clothes, expressions, lighting, posture, size, texture, visual effects)
        - The rule is: "If I want a detail to appear, I must write it explicitly in the prompt"
      </details>
      <example>
        ❌ WRONG: "A throne is in the center of the room."
        ✅ CORRECT: "A massive black stone throne is in the center of the room. Its surface is covered with luminous cracks of spectral blue glow. The sides of the throne have carved details in the shape of interwoven skulls, and its base is elevated by ancient steps worn by time."
      </example>
    </rule_2>

    <rule_3>Rigorous Repetition for Visual Consistency
      <details>
        - Whenever a visual element has appeared before, it must be described AGAIN with all its details
        - Nothing can be assumed. The AI model DOES NOT KNOW WHAT HAS BEEN GENERATED and needs to receive the same descriptions every time
      </details>
      <example>
        ❌ WRONG: "The entity raises its hand."
        ✅ CORRECT: "The colossal entity of 3 meters height, surrounded by intense and blinding golden light, with giant incandescent eyes, wearing an undulating black cloak made of living shadows, slowly raises its gigantic hand, whose fingers seem to absorb the very light around them."
      </example>
    </rule_3>

    <rule_4>Environment and Lighting Must Be Consistent
      <details>
        - The scenery must always be described again in each scene, ensuring that the AI recreates the same visual space
        - If lighting changes, it must be explicitly detailed how this change occurs
      </details>
      <example>
        ❌ WRONG: "Now the environment seems darker."
        ✅ CORRECT: "The dark mist around becomes even denser, partially covering the polished black marble floor. Small golden fragments continue floating in the air, but now appear more sparse, while the distant shadows grow and move slowly, as if they were taking shape."
      </example>
    </rule_4>

    <rule_5>Response Structure Always Follows Sequential Logic
      <sequence>
        1. Character (Always describing each detail again)
        2. Entity or Supernatural Beings (Always detailing with precision)
        3. Environment and Lighting (Repeating the complete description in each scene)
        4. Recurring Elements (Frames, relics, shadows, clocks, etc.)
        5. Scene Changes (Explaining in a totally visual and precise way what happens)
      </sequence>
    </rule_5>
  </fundamental_rules>
