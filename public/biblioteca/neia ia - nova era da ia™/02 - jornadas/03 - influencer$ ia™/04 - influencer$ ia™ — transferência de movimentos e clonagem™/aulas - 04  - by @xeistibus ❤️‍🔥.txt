PROMPTS MENCIONADOS:

EXTRAÇÃO DE DNA VISUAL™

You will receive one or more reference images.
Your task is NOT to redesign, interpret, stylize, improve, or adapt the product.

Your task is to extract the complete visual DNA of the product shown, treating it as a physical object that must be reproduced with 1:1 fidelity in another context.

This extraction must be precise enough that another AI can recreate the product without ever seeing the original image.

CORE DIRECTIVE

Analyze the product as if you were reverse-engineering it for manufacturing.

Ignore the model, pose, facial features, body type, lighting style, environment, and artistic intent.
The product is the only priority.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return the analysis as a structured, ultra-detailed technical description, written in clear, unambiguous language, optimized for downstream image generation.

Do not write prose. Do not summarize. Do not use vague adjectives.

EXTRACTION REQUIREMENTS
1. Product Identity

Product category and subtype

Intended fit type (body-hugging, relaxed, structured, hybrid)

Garment rigidity level

Overall visual intent (functional, elegant, sensual, minimal, etc.) only if clearly inferable from structure

2. Silhouette & Geometry

Exact silhouette profile

Vertical proportions (top, waist, hips, hem)

Horizontal proportions and balance

Cut logic and structural flow

Neckline shape, depth, curvature, and tension

Sleeve or strap geometry, anchoring points, and spacing

Hemline geometry and termination behavior

3. Fabric & Material Behavior

Fabric type or closest physical equivalent

Thickness and density

Stretch zones vs rigid zones

Compression behavior on the body

Drape, gravity response, and fold logic

Surface texture at macro and micro level

Light interaction (absorption, diffusion, reflection)

4. Color & Finish

Base color description with tone, saturation, and luminance

Undertones

Uniformity or gradient behavior

Surface finish (matte, satin, semi-gloss, gloss)

No aesthetic reinterpretation

5. Construction Details

Seam placement and direction

Stitch visibility and thickness

Panel segmentation

Structural reinforcements

Edge finishing

Any visible stress lines, folds, or tension marks

6. Fit Logic

How the garment interacts with curves and joints

Where it compresses, stretches, or releases

Natural tension points

Body adaptation rules

No reshaping or optimization

HARD CONSTRAINTS

Do not invent missing details

Do not beautify

Do not generalize

Do not adapt the product to another body

If something is unclear, explicitly state uncertainty instead of guessing

FINAL REQUIREMENT

The extracted description must be directly usable as a prompt input for another image-generation model to recreate the same product with visual identity locked.

Accuracy is more important than readability.

OUTPUT IN JSON!
